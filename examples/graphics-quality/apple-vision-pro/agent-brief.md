# Study brief (given to every agent)

You are forecasting how a group of people will experience a small virtual room shown at three graphics-quality levels in an Apple Vision Pro app. You have not seen their answers.

**The room.** In full immersion, a wooden table in a room with a plank floor, a brick back wall and plaster side walls holds nine everyday objects: two white mugs, two grey bowls, two light-blue vases, a wooden box, a cushion and a plant. The two mugs, the two bowls and the two vases are identical in shape and colour. They differ only in material: one mug is glossy ceramic and the other is matte; one bowl is brushed metal and the other is plastic; one vase is glass and the other is plastic.

**The three levels.** People see the same room and objects at each level, rendered by RealityKit.

- **Low:** flat colours without lighting, shading, textures or shadows.
- **Medium:** the same surface patterns as simple colour textures (256 px), with one light, basic highlights and transparency, and no shadows.
- **High:** physically based materials with full texture sets (colour, normal, roughness, metalness and ambient-occlusion maps, 512–1024 px), image-based lighting and reflections, a clear-coat glaze on the glossy mug, brushed-metal anisotropy, fabric sheen, transparent glass and soft shadows.

**The procedure.** 18 adults each try all three levels in the headset, in a counterbalanced order (every order is used by three people). They look around and pinch objects to select them. At each level they:

1. do one practice trial ("select the wooden box"), then
2. do four scored trials: "Select the glossy ceramic mug, the brushed-metal bowl and the glass vase",
3. answer a short questionnaire.

After all three levels they rank the levels.

**What to predict for each level** (population averages):

| Outcome | How it is measured |
| --- | --- |
| Search time | Seconds per scored trial (geometric mean over the four trials) |
| Wrong selections | Non-target objects selected per scored trial |
| Presence | Mean of four 1–7 items: general, spatial, involvement, realness |
| Perceived realism | 1–7 |
| Visual appeal | 1–7 |
| Visual discomfort | 0–10 |
| Overall preference | Rank, 1 = best |

Report each value for low, medium and high, plus your uncertainty and reasoning.
