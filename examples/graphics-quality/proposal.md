# Graphics quality and subjective experience in a WebXR room

Research question: How does the graphics quality of a virtual environment (low, medium, high) affect presence, perceived realism, visual appeal, visual discomfort, preference and a material-search task?
Design: within-subjects
Participants: 18
Conditions: Low quality; Medium quality; High quality
Task: In a small room, select the glossy ceramic mug, the brushed-metal bowl and the glass vase among look-alike objects that differ only in material.
Objective outcomes: Search time; Wrong selections
Subjective outcomes: Presence; Perceived realism; Visual appeal; Visual discomfort; Overall preference

Each participant does one practice trial and four scored trials per quality level, then answers a short questionnaire. The order of levels is fully counterbalanced: all six orders, three participants each. After the session, participants rank the three levels.

This proposal is invented for the toy example. It contains no human data.
