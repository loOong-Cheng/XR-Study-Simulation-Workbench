# Building model (synthetic example project)

- **Building:** a three-storey office building, 42 m × 18 m, with open-plan areas, meeting rooms and a stairwell. Participants start on the ground floor near the stairwell.
- **Temperature field:** simulated indoor air temperature on a 25 cm grid, 19–27 °C. Warm spots come from sun-facing windows and server rooms; cool spots from the north façade and air inlets.
- **Occlusion:** walls and floors hide the visualization behind them. To see inside a room, participants enter it or look through glass partitions.
- **Scale:** 1 Unreal unit = 1 cm. Ceiling height is 3 m.
