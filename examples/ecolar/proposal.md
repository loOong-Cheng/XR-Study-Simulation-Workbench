# Building-scale temperature visualization in VR

Research question: How do four VR temperature visualization designs (Spheres, Volume, Probe Plane, Handheld Probe) differ in task performance and subjective experience, and how well do LLM agents with different access forecast these differences at population and individual level?
Design: within-subjects
Participants: 24
Conditions: Spheres (SP); Volume (VO); Probe Plane (PP); Handheld Probe (HP)
Task: In a virtual three-storey office building, compare the temperature of two marked places (8 trials) and find the warmest zone of a floor (4 trials) per design, after three practice trials (two place comparisons, one zone question).
Objective outcomes: Place-comparison accuracy; Zone accuracy; Place-comparison time; Zone time
Subjective outcomes: Confidence after each trial; Workload; BeauVis aesthetic pleasure; Simulator sickness (SSQ); Final rankings for places, zones, interaction, building overview and overall

The order of designs follows a 4 × 4 Williams square, with six participants per order. After each design, participants answer the workload, BeauVis and SSQ questionnaires. After all four designs, they rank the designs on five questions (1 = best).

This proposal is a synthetic example derived from the study design. It contains no human data.
