# Comparing XR selection methods

Research question: How do ray selection and direct touch differ in performance and subjective experience?
Design: within-subjects
Participants: 12
Conditions: Ray selection; Direct touch
Task: Select and place the highlighted target at three distances.
Objective outcomes: Submit time; Selection error rate
Subjective outcomes: Workload; Interaction preference

Participants complete practice before each main block. Record actual condition orders separately. Units, aggregation, questionnaire coding, engine, headset and analysis families must be confirmed during review. This is an invented proposal, with no human data or target answers.
